## 1. Executive Summary
This audit inspects the existing Adorca 360 codebase, route structure, visual aesthetics, component architecture, and content validity to guide the v3 premium transformation.

- **`/robots.txt` & `/sitemap.xml`**: Fully configured for crawl efficiency and search indexation.

## 3. Design System & Visual Aesthetics Audit
- **Palette**: Strict 70% light/neutral surfaces (`#F7F8FA` / `#FFFFFF`), 20% deep navy cinematic moments (`#081033`), 10% electric blue (`#1657FF`) and lime green (`#98F22A`) accents.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk display headings + Inter body font with clamp-based fluid scaling.
- **Motion System**: Transform and opacity-driven animations with comprehensive `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` fallbacks.
