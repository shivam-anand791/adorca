# Adorca 360 — Design Audit

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Aesthetic Evaluation

### Current State vs Target State
- **Brand Positioning**: Premium international digital growth studio (combining technical SEO, programmatic media, content architecture, and conversion engineering across 50+ markets).
- **Visual Personality**: Editorial + Data-driven + Cinematic + Intelligent.
- **Color Balance**:
  - **70% Light / Neutral Surfaces**: Clean, modern canvas (`#F7F8FA`) with elevated crisp white cards (`#FFFFFF`) and dark ink typography (`#101114`).
  - **20% Deep Navy Cinematic Moments**: Dark high-contrast sections (`#081033`) for the signature **Growth System** flow and **Final CTA**.
  - **10% Electric Blue (`#1657FF`) & Lime Green (`#98F22A`) Accents**: High-precision highlights for interactive nodes, trend indicators, and conversion actions.
